# Incident Severity Framework

> Fictional Arcway Cloud framework created for portfolio demonstration. These are not universal industry thresholds.

| Level | Example Impact | Response | Communication |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEV-1 Critical | Widespread outage or severe data/security impact | Immediate incident command and cross-functional response | Approx. 15-minute cadence while active |
| SEV-2 Major | Material degradation affecting multiple customers or a critical workflow | Rapid incident response with clear owner | Approx. 30-minute cadence while material impact continues |
| SEV-3 Moderate | Limited degradation with workaround or constrained scope | Prioritized investigation | Update on meaningful changes |
| SEV-4 Minor | Low-impact defect, isolated issue or informational problem | Standard Support/Product workflow | Normal ticket cadence |

## Classification Principles

Severity reflects verified impact and urgency, not how strongly a ticket is worded. Consider scope, criticality of the affected workflow, workaround availability, data/security implications and evidence of shared platform behavior.

Customer tier can inform relationship handling but should not by itself redefine technical incident severity.

## Reclassification

Severity can change as evidence changes. Document why the classification moved and adjust ownership and communication cadence accordingly.
